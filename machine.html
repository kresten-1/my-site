The useless machine

A machine with one job: it switches itself off. Flip the switch and it flips back. After a few tries it stops sitting still and starts to move.

The machine

Flips: 0 · Escapes: 0

How it works

  • Every click is one flip. The switch never stays on — that is the whole machine.
  • Every third flip, the switch jumps to another place inside the box.
  • Start again puts the switch back and sets both counts to zero.

A little history

The useless machine was a toy sold in the 1960s: a box with a switch, and a small arm that reached out and turned the switch off again. It does nothing, and that is the point.
